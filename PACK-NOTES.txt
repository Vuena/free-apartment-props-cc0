MODULAR APARTMENT INTERIOR KIT - FREE SAMPLE (CC0)
====================================================

Thank you for grabbing this sample! It is 14 ready-to-use models from the full Modular Apartment Interior Kit, free for any project, commercial or not.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  Living: coffee table, table lamp, small potted plant, round side table, floor lamp, potted cactus
  Clutter: mug, square cushion, vase with twigs, stack of books, wicker basket
  Bedroom: nightstand (the drawer is a separate part)
  Kitchen: dining chair, kettle

FILES
  GLB/        self-contained (textures embedded), one file per model
  FBX/        same models as FBX; they read textures from ../../Textures (keep the folder layout)
  Textures/   12 texture files used by these models
  Preview/    the cover image
  LICENSE-FREE-SAMPLE-CC0.txt

NOTES
  1 unit = 1 metre. Front is -Y in Blender. Pivots are at the bottom centre of each piece.
  FBX files do not carry the packed roughness/metal maps (a Blender FBX exporter limit); the GLB files have everything.
  Every model was modelled by code in Blender (Python scripts); no AI-generated meshes or textures. Checked by script, not opened in Unity, Unreal or the Godot editor.
